AGENTIC · MARTECH

Next-Best-Action Orchestrator

5 agents · 3 safety layers

Cart-recovery and renewal marketing usually runs on one static reminder rule. I replaced it with a team of five Claude agents that decide, per shopper cohort, when to reach out, what to offer, and on which channel. Every decision passes through three independent safety layers before anything is sent.

Three safety layers, each a different mechanism

1 · ReasoningLLM Critic

Approves or rejects each decision with a concrete suggested fix. After two failed rounds the action drops to "wait" or "escalate" with confidence capped at 0.4.

2 · CodeDeterministic guardrail

Plain Python enforces a 20% discount ceiling, SMS consent, PII kept onsite, and quiet hours from 21:00 to 08:00. Every change is logged.

3 · PeopleHuman-in-the-loop

A reviewer signs off whenever confidence is below 0.6 or the cart is worth $150 or more, through a daily review console.

Engineering

  • Two interchangeable engines with one contract: plain Python, and a LangGraph StateGraph with durable checkpointing, crash-resume and a native human-review interrupt.
  • Idempotent decisions keyed on visitor and run date, plus a cross-day 24-hour frequency cap.
  • Built-in scoreboard comparing agent decisions against the static rule on recovered carts, revenue, discount cost and net.

Deployment

  • Databricks-native: a Workflow reads gold events from Unity Catalog, agents run on Mosaic AI Model Serving, state lives in Delta tables, and a Streamlit Databricks App hosts the review console.
  • Shipped with Databricks Asset Bundles. Braze is the only external hop, running in shadow mode first.
  • A full AWS design also exists: ECS Fargate, SQS with DLQ, DynamoDB checkpoints, and Bedrock Batch for non-urgent decisions at about half the cost.

DATA · STREAMING

First-Party Web Events Pipeline

15M events/day · 500K/min peak

An end-to-end, first-party pipeline that collects web events in the browser and streams them into a Databricks medallion architecture for the customer data platform, with personal data kept separate from the moment it is collected.

Browser SDK

  • Dependency-free JavaScript: batching, retry with exponential backoff that honors 429 Retry-After, a three-tier unload fallback, dedup and a schema allowlist.
  • Tested with about 717 lines of Jest unit tests and 168 lines of Playwright end-to-end tests.

Streaming layer

  • Spark Structured Streaming on Change Data Feed with immutable facts: insert-only MERGE gives silent dedup on message ID.
  • Bot filtering, two backward-compatible Avro schemas, and a production runbook with InfoSec and Legal sign-off and a 30-minute rollback.
